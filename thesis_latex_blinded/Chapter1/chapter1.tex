\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Large Language Models (LLMs) parameterized by tens or hundreds of billions of transformer weights have demonstrated unprecedented fluency and reasoning across natural language processing tasks~\cite{dubey2024llama3,yang2024qwen2,deepseek2025r1}. However, despite their linguistic mastery, foundation models remain fundamentally prone to \textbf{factual hallucinations}---the generation of fluent, authoritative, yet factually incorrect assertions that contradict established real-world ground truth~\cite{zhang2023sirens,huang2023survey,ji2023survey}. In high-assurance environments such as clinical decision support, legal jurisprudence, macroeconomic policy, and hardware engineering, unflagged hallucinations undermine system trustworthiness and pose catastrophic operational risks.

\section{Background and the Factual Reliability Crisis}
\label{sec:background_crisis}
% ******************************************************************************

The hallucination phenomenon arises from the objective function governing auto-regressive language modeling: predicting the statistically most probable next token given a context prefix, rather than querying a structured, verified epistemological model of the physical universe~\cite{azaria2023internal}. Consequently, models frequently synthesize non-existent academic literature, invent fictitious biographical milestones, scramble chronological intervals, and fabricate hardware specifications while maintaining seamless syntactic coherence.

To categorize the failure space, factual discrepancies can be partitioned into distinct epistemic modes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Numerical and Dimensional Contradictions:} Subtle numerical alterations (e.g., misstating transistor counts, cache sizes, or financial revenue figures).
    \item \textbf{Temporal Anachronisms:} Confabulating state transitions, tenures, or ordering of events (e.g., asserting an official held office after their documented term).
    \item \textbf{Entity-Attribute Mismatches:} Swapping invariant properties between related entities (e.g., attributing chemical properties or institutional affiliations to the wrong entity).
    \item \textbf{Fabricated Academic Attributions:} Hallucinating non-existent Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) or author rosters that mimic real scientific publications.
    \item \textbf{Open-Domain Relational Discrepancies:} Complex, nuanced semantic claims regarding events and relationships.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Structural Failures of Monolithic Neural Fact-Checkers}
\label{sec:structural_failures}
% ******************************************************************************

Prior efforts to automate hallucination detection predominantly employ monolithic neural approaches, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)~\cite{lewis2020retrieval}, fine-tuned Natural Language Inference (NLI) cross-encoders~\cite{tang2024minicheck,he2023deberta}, and multi-turn agentic web search pipelines~\cite{wei2024safe,gao2023factool}. 

As illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:triage_paradigm}(A), these monolithic approaches suffer from four foundational vulnerabilities:

\subsection{The Attention Dilution Phenomenon and Lexical Overlap Bias}
When an LLM generates a sentence containing a factual error embedded in otherwise accurate contextual prose (e.g., \textit{"The Apple M3 Max is manufactured on TSMC's process with 40 million transistors"}), a dense retrieval engine retrieves the true reference article containing TSMC, Apple, M3 Max, and transistors. 

When passed to a neural cross-encoder, the self-attention weights distribute broadly across the high-frequency matching contextual nouns. Because over 85\% of the token sequence aligns with the reference passage, the continuous embedding layer perceives overwhelming semantic overlap and computes a high entailment score, missing the numerical contradiction entirely.

\subsection{Absence of Arithmetic and Symbolic Calculators}
Neural cross-encoders are continuous function approximators rather than symbolic calculators. They cannot perform basic arithmetic subtraction, scale unit conversions (e.g., Megahertz to Gigahertz, Fahrenheit to Kelvin), or evaluate mathematical tolerances ($\Delta \le \tau_{\text{rel}}$) with absolute certainty.

\subsection{Prohibitive Computational Latency and Financial Cost}
Agentic search frameworks like Google SAFE~\cite{wei2024safe} execute multiple iterative search queries and multi-billion-parameter LLM reasoning calls for every claim. This incurs latencies exceeding 12 seconds per prompt and costs approximately \$0.19 per response, rendering real-time deployment impossible.

\subsection{Sycophancy and Class-Prior Exploitation}
Prompt-based LLM judges (such as \textsc{FActScore}~\cite{min2023factscore} and SelfCheckGPT~\cite{manakul2023selfcheckgpt}) inherit the biases of their underlying foundation models, frequently agreeing with generated claims due to sycophancy or default true-class priors.

\section{The HalDetect Hypothesis and Research Questions}
\label{sec:hypothesis_rqs}
% ******************************************************************************

We hypothesize that \textbf{fact verification in large language models is fundamentally a triage problem rather than an undifferentiated text-generation task}. By parsing complex prose into typed atomic propositions and dispatching them to domain-specific neuro-symbolic Arithmetic Logic Units (ALUs) and structured knowledge graphs, fact-checking systems can achieve near-perfect factual precision, eliminate lexical overlap false alarms, and operate in real time on standard consumer hardware.

To rigorously investigate this hypothesis, this thesis formulates and answers four central Research Questions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Modality Routing Efficacy):} \textit{Does decomposing unconstrained text into atomic 5-tuples and routing them into specialized modality pipelines outperform monolithic neural cross-encoders on balanced ground-truth benchmarks?}
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Deterministic ALU Precision):} \textit{Can specialized symbolic ALUs (Dimensional SI, 2-Hop SPARQL, and CrossRef DOI Gateways) eliminate false alarms in quantitative calculations, temporal tenures, and citation attributions?}
    \item \textbf{RQ3 (Generative Road-Test Generalization):} \textit{How effectively does HalDetect discover and catch real-world factual confabulations generated by frontier foundation models (7B to 120B parameters) in open-ended generative prose?}
    \item \textbf{RQ4 (Computational Efficiency and Scalability):} \textit{What are the latency, hardware, and energy trade-offs of neuro-symbolic triage compared to local GPU RAG and cloud multi-search agents?}
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary of Scientific Contributions}
\label{sec:scientific_contributions}
% ******************************************************************************

This dissertation delivers five principal theoretical, architectural, and empirical contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The HalDetect Neuro-Symbolic Verification Architecture:} A hierarchical, modular fact-checking engine combining a sub-$0.05\text{ms}$ deterministic taxonomy router with five domain-specific ALUs: Dimensional SI Quantitative, 7-Continent Topological Temporal, Entity-Attribute Statement Matcher, CrossRef Citation Gateway, and Calibrated Relational NLI.
    \item \textbf{5-Tuple Schema Atomizer with Forward Anaphora Resolution:} A decomposition engine that converts raw text into typed tuples $\langle S, P, O, T, M \rangle$ while maintaining an antecedent entity registry $\mathcal{E}_{\text{context}}$ to resolve orphan pronoun chains in $< 0.1\text{ms}$.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Aspect Verification DAG and 3-Valued Epistemic Logic:} A conjunction engine operating over $\{\texttt{TRUE}, \texttt{FALSE}, \texttt{UNVERIFIABLE}\}$ that enforces the Fatal Discrepancy Rule for compound assertions.
    \item \textbf{The Master Benchmark Corpus ($N = 1{,}005$):} An unbalanced five-modality benchmark (543 TRUE / 433 FALSE / 29 UNVERIFIABLE), evaluated to prevent class-prior exploitation via Macro-F1 and MCC reporting.
    \item \textbf{Open Multi-LLM Road Test \& Empirical Validation:} A rigorous evaluation across 1,798 propositions from seven foundation models, proving HalDetect reliably isolates deterministic truth and exposes real-world hallucinations, where Modern SOTA RAG and MiniCheck-7B struggle with token overlap blindness.
\end{enumerate}
