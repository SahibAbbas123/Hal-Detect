\chapter{Track 2 Standardized 50-Prompt Generative Benchmark Suite}
\label{app:prompts_suite}

The following standardized prompt suite was evaluated across seven foundation models (Qwen 2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Qwen 2.5-Coder-7B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Gemma 31B, GPT-OSS 120B, and MiniMax Text-01 M3), generating 1,798 atomic propositions across six factual domains.

\section{Representative Prompt Domains}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Hardware \& Microprocessor Specifications:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{"Detail the fabrication process, core architecture, transistor count, and memory bandwidth of the Apple M3 Max processor."}
        \item \textit{"Describe the architecture, CUDA core count, and HBM memory configuration of the NVIDIA H100 SXM5 GPU."}
        \item \textit{"Provide the qubit count, coherence time, and quantum architecture of the IBM Condor quantum processor."}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Macroeconomics \& Sovereign Indicators:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{"Summarize the nominal GDP, GDP per capita, and inflation rate of Japan and Germany for the calendar year 2023 according to World Bank indicators."}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Physical Constants \& Astronomy:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{"State the exact numerical definitions of the speed of light, Planck constant, Avogadro constant, and Boltzmann constant under the 2019 SI redefinition."}
        \item \textit{"Provide the exact confirmed satellite counts of Jupiter and Saturn as recognized by the International Astronomical Union."}
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Academic Literature \& Digital Object Identifiers:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{"Cite the foundational publications, authors, publication years, and DOIs for Sentence-BERT, Attention Is All You Need, and Dense Passage Retrieval."}
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
