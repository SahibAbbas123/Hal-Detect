\section{Summary of Scientific Contributions}
\label{sec:conclusion_summary}
% ******************************************************************************

This dissertation established that fact verification in large language models is fundamentally a \textbf{modality-triage challenge} rather than an undifferentiated sequence-to-sequence text generation task. By designing HalDetect, a type-directed neuro-symbolic verification architecture, we demonstrated that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Symbolic Triage Outperforms Monolithic Cross-Encoders:} Routing propositions into specialized arithmetic, chronological, and citation pipelines improves selective accuracy from $60.00\%$ to \textbf{97.60\%} ($\text{MCC} = +0.9520$) on balanced ground truth ($p < 10^{-60}$).
    \item \textbf{Eradication of the Lexical Overlap Trap:} While modern neural RAG and MiniCheck-7B cross-encoders failed to catch any of the 31 real-world generative confabulations produced by foundation models ($0.0\%$ recall), HalDetect's deterministic ALUs caught all \textbf{31 out of 31 errors} ($100.0\%$ recall).
    \item \textbf{Deterministic Routing Beats Frontier LLMs:} A lightweight symbolic router achieves \textbf{88.80\% accuracy in $< 0.05\text{ms}$}, avoiding the 100\% relational mode collapse and citation blindness exhibited by 120B-parameter LLMs.
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Efficiency on Consumer Hardware:} HalDetect verifies claims in \textbf{24.3 ms per claim on a standard laptop CPU}, operating 24$\times$ faster than local GPU RAG and 500$\times$ faster than cloud search agents at zero marginal cost.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Released Artifacts and Reproducibility Suite}
\label{sec:released_artifacts}
% ******************************************************************************

To support reproducibility and community evaluation, all code, benchmark datasets, prompt suites, and evaluation logs are released under open-source licenses:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Core Verification ALUs:} Complete implementations of:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \path{schema_atomizer.py} (5-tuple decomposition and anaphora resolution).
        \item \path{dimensional_quantity_engine.py} (SI unit conversion and discrepancy).
        \item \path{bidirectional_kg_resolver.py} (7-continent topological SPARQL traversal).
        \item \path{multi_aspect_dag_verifier.py} (3-valued conjunction engine).
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Symmetrical Master Benchmark Corpus ($N=1,000$):} Symmetrical 500-TRUE / 500-FALSE dataset annotated across five modalities (\path{haldetect_master_benchmark_1000.csv}).
    \item \textbf{Track 2 Multi-LLM Benchmark Suite:} 1,798 annotated propositions generated across seven foundation models from 50 standardized prompts (\path{unified_7model_roadtest_benchmark.csv}).
    \item \textbf{Mechanical AST Leak-Check Audit:} Automated static analysis script (\path{honest_ablation_runner.py}) guaranteeing zero evaluation leakage.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Research Directions}
\label{sec:future_directions}
% ******************************************************************************

\subsection{Hybrid GraphRAG Integration}
While HalDetect excels at structured, single-hop and 2-hop assertions against curated knowledge bases, complex multi-hop narrative reasoning across unstructured documents remains an open frontier. Integrating graph-traversal indexing (e.g., HippoRAG~\cite{gutierrez2024hipporag}) as a specialized sub-engine within HalDetect's Relational pipeline represents a compelling hybrid evolution.

\subsection{Multimodal Vision-Language Verification}
Extending the 5-tuple proposition schema to multimodal visual grounding---verifying whether generated image captions or visual descriptions match bounding boxes and spatial coordinates---would generalize neuro-symbolic verification to multimodal foundation models.

\section{Concluding Remarks}
\label{sec:concluding_remarks}
% ******************************************************************************

As language models are increasingly integrated into critical infrastructure, ensuring factual veracity cannot rely on the same probabilistic heuristics that produce hallucinations. HalDetect demonstrates that anchoring language models to deterministic, symbolic, and calibrated knowledge structures provides a scalable, auditable, and mathematically sound pathway toward trustworthy artificial intelligence.
